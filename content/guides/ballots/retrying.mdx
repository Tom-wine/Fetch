# Retrying a run

By the end of this page you will know exactly what RETRY_FAILED does, what it carries
over, and when retrying makes things worse.

## What it does

**RETRY_FAILED (N)** creates a **new run** containing exactly the accounts that ended
`FAILED` in the old one.

The original run is not touched. It keeps its counts, its log and its outcome, because it
is the record of what happened — a retry that rewrote history would destroy the only
evidence of why you retried.

The new run:

- contains the failed accounts and nothing else;
- uses **the same profile**, with whatever settings it has *now*;
- is labelled after the original — `Away scheme — retry`;
- starts immediately, and you land on its monitor.

Available from the monitor's header and from the row menu in the run history.

## What it does not carry over

- **Accounts that ended `NEEDS_OTP`.** They did not fail; they stopped at a challenge.
  Attach a mailbox or clear the code, then start a normal run.
- **Accounts that ended `SKIPPED`.** Nothing was submitted for them. Include them in a
  fresh run instead.
- **The original's timing.** The estimate is recomputed for the smaller set.

The button is disabled when nothing failed, and says so: "Nothing in this run failed."

## Fix first, then retry

The profile carries forward — which means retrying without changing anything reproduces
the conditions that caused the failures.

| The failures said | Do this first |
| --- | --- |
| `RATE_LIMITED` | Lower the concurrency and raise the delay on the profile, or duplicate it into a slower one and use that. |
| `PROXY_DEAD` | Test the proxy group and replace the dead address. |
| `BLOCKED` | Sign in to the club site by hand for those accounts, then check them. |
| `OTP_TIMEOUT` | Set the profile's OTP source to IMAP and attach a mailbox. |
| `UPSTREAM_ERROR` | Nothing. Retry. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Retrying into a rate limit">
This is the mistake worth naming. Forty accounts come back `RATE_LIMITED`; pressing
RETRY_FAILED sends the same forty at the same speed to a club that has just told you to
slow down. The second run fails faster than the first, and the club now has two bursts to
look at instead of one.
</Callout>

## When retrying is the wrong move

- **When the club is having an outage.** `UPSTREAM_ERROR` on every account is not forty
  problems, it is one. Wait.
- **When the ballot has closed.** A retry after the window is a run of confirmed
  failures.
- **When the failures are `SKIPPED`.** The membership is not eligible; a second attempt
  will not change the club's mind.
- **When you have not read a single failure message.** Retrying is a decision that
  should follow evidence, and the evidence is one click away in `LAST_RESPONSE`.

## Doing it by hand instead

Sometimes the right answer is a normal run rather than a retry — for instance when you
want the failures **and** the accounts you fixed since:

1. Go to **01_pool**.
2. Filter or select the accounts you want.
3. **START_RUN** with the selection as the scope.

The launcher's `SELECTION` option exists for exactly this.

## Reading the retry afterwards

The retry is its own run in the history, with its own log and its own outcome. Compare
the two: if the same accounts fail the same way with a slower profile, the problem is not
pace — it is those accounts, and the answer is on
[the failure codes page](/guides/ballots/failure-codes).
