# Quick start

By the end of this page you will have entered ten accounts into a ballot and watched
every attempt land. It takes about five minutes.

You need ten club accounts and their passwords. Nothing else has to be set up first.

## 1. Load the accounts

Open **//ballot_entries** in the sidebar and stay on **01_pool**.

At the top is the paste loader. Choose the club these accounts belong to, then paste one
account per line:

```
a.hughes@mail.com:W1nterGr0ve!
j.moreau@mail.com:CoteDazur#22
k.novak@mail.com:Brno-1997-Zima
```

A colon between the address and the password is the usual separator; a comma or a
semicolon works too. Press **LOAD_ACCOUNTS** and they appear in the table below.

![The pool tab, with the paste loader above the table of accounts](/guides/pool.png)

<Callout type="note">
The block you paste is sent once and never stored — not in the URL, not in local
storage, not in the console. The field clears the moment the accounts are created.
</Callout>

Accounts already in the pool are counted and skipped rather than duplicated, so pasting
the same block twice is harmless. If you have a spreadsheet instead, use
[the CSV import](/guides/accounts/loading-accounts) — it maps your columns to ours and
shows you every row it could not read before anything is written.

## 2. Check who is ready

Look at the **READY** column and the counter beside START_RUN. It reads something like
`20 of 48 ready`.

READY means the account can enter a ballot right now: the session is valid, the
membership is live, and it has whatever the profile needs. BLOCKED accounts say why in
one word — `no proxy`, `needs login`, `membership expired`.

You do not have to fix them to continue. The launcher leaves them out and tells you it
did.

## 3. Start the run

Press **START_RUN**. The launcher opens.

![The launcher, with clubs, scope, profile, estimate and label](/guides/launcher.png)

Four things to look at, top to bottom:

- **Scope** — the clubs, and how many accounts are going. It defaults to ALL_READY.
- **Profile** — leave it on **Default** for now: eight at once, two to five seconds
  apart, two retries.
- **Estimate** — how long this will take, from the count and the profile. Read it. It is
  the one number that catches "I meant ten accounts, not three hundred".
- **Label** — optional. Left blank, the run is named after its clubs and the time.

Press **START_RUN (N)**. You land on the monitor.

## 4. Read the monitor

![The run monitor: counters, progress bar, task table and log](/guides/monitor.png)

The band across the top answers "is it working" without reading anything else:

![The stats band: seven counters over one segmented progress bar](/guides/monitor-band.png)

- **SUCCESS** — the club confirmed the entry.
- **FAILED** — the club refused it. The reason is in the table.
- **NEEDS_OTP** — a code was asked for and never arrived.
- **QUEUED** — not attempted yet.

Every counter is a filter. Click **FAILED** and the table below shows only the failures;
click it again to clear.

In the table, **LAST_RESPONSE** is the column that matters. It carries the club's status
code and a sentence saying what happened, in English:

> The proxy stopped responding mid-entry. Nothing was submitted.

<Callout type="note" title="You can leave">
The run keeps going if you navigate away, and a strip in the top bar follows you around
the app with its progress and a link back. Closing the tab does not stop it either — the
run lives on the backend, not in your browser.
</Callout>

## 5. Deal with the failures

When it finishes, press **RETRY_FAILED (N)** in the header. That starts a NEW run
containing exactly the accounts that failed, leaving the original as a record of what
happened. [More on retrying](/guides/ballots/retrying).

Before you retry, read one failure. `RATE_LIMITED` means the profile is too fast for
that club and retrying at the same settings will fail the same way;
[the failure codes page](/guides/ballots/failure-codes) says which is which.
